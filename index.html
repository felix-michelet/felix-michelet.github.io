Félix Michelet

Post-doctoral researcher in economics, University of Cologne

I am a post-doctoral researcher in economics at the University of Cologne, Faculty of Management, Economics and Social Sciences. My research focuses on the market effects of renewable energy deployment and the ex-post evaluation of energy and environmental policies.

My work examines how the expansion of wind and solar power affects electricity markets and greenhouse gas emissions, with particular attention to market integration, carbon taxation, and the distributional consequences of environmental regulation. I rely on modern microeconometric tools for causal identification and policy analysis.

My job market paper was awarded the 2nd prize by the French Association of Environmental and Resource Economists (FAERE) Young Economist Award.
